Canal Grande di Trieste 4 - High 0.2 N/A (Public Canal) A picturesque 18th-century navigable canal surrounded by historic palaces, cafes, and historic churches. Buffet da Pepi, Osteria I Ferri
Roman Theatre 5 - High 0.1 N/A (Historical Site) Striking remains of a 1st-century Roman amphitheater integrated right into the middle of the modern city streets. Al Bagatto, Chimera di Bacco
Grotta Gigante 6 - Medium 6.0 grottagigante.it One of the largest tourist caves in the world, featuring massive stalactites and stalagmites. Osteria de l'Oca (nearby Opicina)
Risiera di San Sabba 7 - Medium 2.5 risierasansabba.it A former rice-husking facility that became a WWII concentration camp; now a deeply moving museum and memorial. Antica Trattoria Suban
