Piazza dell'Unità d'Italia 10/10 (Must-See) 0.0 turismofvg.it/piazza-unita Known as Europe's largest sea-facing square, featuring magnificent 19th-century Austro-Hungarian architecture. Harry's Piccolo, Caffè degli Specchi, Al Bagatto
Miramare Castle 10/10 (Must-See) 5.0 castello-miramare.it A stunning 19th-century white stone castle built on a cliff over the sea for Archduke Ferdinand Maximilian of Habsburg. Le Vele, Ristorante Principe di Metternich
Castello di San Giusto 9/10 (Highly Recommended) 0.5 castellodisangiustotrieste.it A 15th-century fortress offering panoramic views of the city and the Gulf of Trieste, housing a historical museum. Antica Trattoria Suban, Trattoria Nerodiseppia
Canal Grande di Trieste 9/10 (Highly Recommended) 0.2 turismofvg.it/canal-grande A picturesque navigable waterway built in the 18th century to let boats reach the city center, lined with cafes and palaces. Vecio Buffet Marascutti, Caffè Stella Polare
Roman Theatre 8/10 (Important) 0.2 turismofvg.it/teatro-romano Impressive ancient Roman amphitheater dating back to the 1st century AD, nestled at the foot of San Giusto hill. Buffet da Pepi, Chimera di Bacco
Grotta Gigante 8/10 (Important) 8.0 grottagigante.it One of the largest tourist caves in the world, featuring massive stalactites and stalagmites in the Italian Karst region. Trattoria Rampa, Ristorante Falesie
Risiera di San Sabba 7/10 (Historical Significance) 3.0 risierasansabba.it A former rice-husking facility that was converted into a WWII concentration camp; now a solemn national monument and museum. Pizzeria Il Melone, Trattoria da Giovanni
Cattedrale di San Giusto 7/10 (Historical/Cultural) 0.5 turismofvg.it/cattedrale Trieste's main Catholic cathedral, created by joining two older churches, featuring beautiful 12th-century Byzantine mosaics. Hostaria G.I., Trattoria Nerodiseppia
