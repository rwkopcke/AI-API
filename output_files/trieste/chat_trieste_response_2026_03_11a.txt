A few quick planning tips
- Getting your bearings: I used Piazza Unità d’Italia as the center point for all distances. For out‑of‑town sights (Miramare, Grotta Gigante), plan bus/taxi time; Miramare is roughly 8 km/5 mi from the square. ([rome2rio.com](https://www.rome2rio.com/s/Castello-di-Miramare/Piazza-dell-Unit%C3%A0-d-Italia?utm_source=openai))
- Noise and breakfast: In Italy, breakfast is often a paid add‑on unless stated otherwise; Urban Hotel Design explicitly includes breakfast; others typically offer it as an optional rate. ([urbanhotel.it](https://www.urbanhotel.it/en/faqs/?utm_source=openai))
